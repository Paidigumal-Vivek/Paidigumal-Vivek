# Presentation Guide

## Executive narrative

The presentation is structured to move from **objective → methodology → baseline workload → temporal/geographic patterns → operational intelligence → recommendations**.

## Slide sequence

1. **Title** — Kent County Sheriff's Office CAD Data Analysis
2. **Objective & Analytical Approach** — assessment objective, validation, feature engineering, and Power BI approach
3. **Executive Operational Assessment** — 71,864 calls, 110 incident types, 26 townships, 7,192 repeat addresses
4. **Incident Type Assessment** — traffic stops and other high-frequency service categories
5. **Spatial & Temporal Workload Assessment** — township concentration and peak demand periods
6. **High-Frequency Location Assessment** — recurring service locations
7. **Beyond the Baseline Analysis** — day, priority, response time, units, call source, and township response comparisons
8. **Geographic Operational Intelligence** — mapping, filtering, and cross-filtering capabilities
9. **Intelligence Assessment & Operational Considerations** — executive synthesis and recommended areas for review

## Recommended presentation flow

- Spend the opening on the operational question rather than technical detail.
- Explain how the data was validated and transformed.
- Present required baseline measures first.
- Use the Beyond the Baseline section to demonstrate independent analytical thinking.
- Finish with operational implications rather than simply restating charts.

## Portfolio emphasis

The strongest portfolio signals are:

- Ability to translate an ambiguous operational question into an analytical framework.
- Data-quality awareness.
- Feature engineering for operational analytics.
- Power BI dashboard design.
- Geographic and temporal analysis.
- Communication of findings to leadership.
- Clear distinction between an analytical signal and a confirmed operational conclusion.
